###### Economic diversity and student outcomes at  the


University of Texas at Dallas
=============================



 Richardson, Texas
 


 The median family income of a student from University of Texas at Dallas is **$89,800**, and **40%** come from the top 20 percent. About **2\.4%** of students at University of Texas at Dallas came from a poor family but became a rich adult.



A [new study](http://www.nytimes.com/interactive/2017/01/18/upshot/some-colleges-have-more-students-from-the-top-1-percent-than-the-bottom-60.html), based on millions of anonymous tax records, shows that some colleges are even more economically segregated than previously understood, while others are associated with income mobility.


Below, estimates of how University of Texas at Dallas compares with its peer schools in economic diversity and student outcomes.
