A database for all official *Scooby\-Doo* media and merchandise

**Scoobypedia** is an encyclopedia on the hit television series *[Scooby\-Doo](/wiki/Scooby-Doo_(franchise) "Scooby-Doo (franchise)")* which has been airing for over **50 years**! 


The show follows the iconic mystery solving detectives, known as [Mystery Inc.](/wiki/Mystery_Inc. "Mystery Inc."), as they set out in [the Mystery Machine](/wiki/Mystery_Machine "Mystery Machine") to solve crime and unmask criminals, bent on revenge or committing criminal acts for their own personal gain.


Titular character, [Scooby](/wiki/Scooby-Doo "Scooby-Doo"), is accompanied by his best pal [Shaggy](/wiki/Shaggy_Rogers "Shaggy Rogers") as both vie for [Scooby Snacks](/wiki/Scooby_Snacks "Scooby Snacks") on their adventures! [Velma](/wiki/Velma_Dinkley "Velma Dinkley") brings her laser\-focused intellect and initiative to collecting clues and solving mysteries. [Fred](/wiki/Fred_Jones "Fred Jones") is the team's leader who devises elaborate traps to catch the bad guys, while [Daphne](/wiki/Daphne_Blake "Daphne Blake") is bold and empathetic and keeps the Gang together.


We are the go\-to encyclopedia on all\-things *Scooby\-Doo* and are [currently editing](/wiki/Special:RecentChanges "Special:RecentChanges") over [18,480](/wiki/Special:AllPages "Special:AllPages") articles \- we need **your** help! [Create an account](/wiki/Special:CreateAccount "Special:CreateAccount"), [Contribute to articles](https://community.fandom.com/wiki/Help:Editing "Help:Editing"), and [discuss the show](https://scoobydoo.fandom.com/f) on the number 1 *Scooby\-Doo* fansite!


  






Velma

The adult\-oriented series focused on [Velma](/wiki/Velma_Dinkley_(Velma) "Velma Dinkley (Velma)") (voiced by Mindy Kaling) returns soon for a Halloween special!


The gang races to bring Velma back from the dead before the clock strikes midnight on Halloween as evil forces descend upon them.


*[This Halloween Needs to be More Special!](/wiki/This_Halloween_Needs_to_be_More_Special! "This Halloween Needs to be More Special!")* will air on MAX on October 3\.

Scooby\-Doo! and Krypto, Too!


Follow the adventures of the Mystery Inc. gang in the all\-new *Scooby\-Doo* movie, *[Scooby\-Doo! and Krypto, Too!](/wiki/Scooby-Doo!_and_Krypto,_Too! "Scooby-Doo! and Krypto, Too!")*  

The worlds of Scooby\-Doo and the DC Superheroes collide when the Justice League disappears, and [Mystery Inc.](/wiki/Mystery_Inc. "Mystery Inc.") is called to Metropolis to investigate. A fiery phantom has taken up residence in an otherwise deserted Hall of Justice, and supervillains like the [Joker](/wiki/Joker "Joker"), [Harley Quinn](/wiki/Harley_Quinn "Harley Quinn"), [Solomon Grundy](/wiki/Solomon_Grundy "Solomon Grundy") and [Lex Luthor](/wiki/Lex_Luthor "Lex Luthor") are running amok. It is up to those meddling kids, and their new pal Krypto the Superdog, to save the day.


The movie is out now on digital and as both [a general release and a Walmart exclusive](/wiki/Scooby-Doo!_and_Krypto,_Too!_(Home_Media) "Scooby-Doo! and Krypto, Too! (Home Media)") DVD.
