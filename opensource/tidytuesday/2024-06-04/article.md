Cheese is nutritious food made mostly from the milk of cows but also other mammals,
 including sheep, goats, buffalo, reindeer, camels and yaks. Around 4000 years ago people
 have started to breed animals and process their milk. That's when the cheese was
 born.


Explore this site to find out about different kinds of cheese from all around the
 world.



 You can search the database of 2037 variaties of cheese
 by [names](/alphabetical/),
 by [country of origin](/by_country/),
 by [kind of milk](/by_milk/) that is used to produce it, or
 by [texture](/by_texture/).
 


The database includes information on most famous sorts of cheese such as
 [Cheddar](/cheddar/),
 [Camembert](/camembert/),
 [Stilton](/stilton/) or
 [Parmesan](/parmesan-parmigiano/), as well as rarities
 like
 [Crottin de Chavignol](/crottin-de-chavignol/).

The most expensive cheese in the world is Pule and it's made from donkey's milk.
