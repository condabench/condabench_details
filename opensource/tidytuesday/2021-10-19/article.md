#### About us

The Great Pumpkin Commonwealth's (GPC) mission cultivates the hobby of growing giant pumpkins and other giant fruits throughout the world by establishing standards and regulations that ensure quality of fruit, fairness of competition, recognition of achievement, fellowship and education for all participating growers and weigh\-off sites.#### Becoming a Weigh\-Off Site

There are many weigh\-off sites around the world and becoming a GPC site is easy.* [Register To Be A Site](https://gpc1.org/weigh-off-site-registration-2023/)
