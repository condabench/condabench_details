Using patty\-power parity to think about exchange rates
-------------------------------------------------------

 Jul 31st 2024ShareTHE BIG MAC index was invented by *The Economist* in 1986 as a lighthearted guide to whether currencies are at their “correct” level. It is based on the theory of purchasing\-power parity (PPP), the notion that in the long run exchange rates should move towards the rate that would equalise the prices of an identical basket of goods and services (in this case, a burger) in any two countries.
