This article is about specific supercentenarian claims validated by modern standards. For the oldest currently living people, see [List of oldest living people](/wiki/List_of_oldest_living_people "List of oldest living people").



These are lists of the 100 known **verified oldest women and men** sorted in descending order by age in years and days.[\[a]](#cite_note-1)


The oldest person ever whose age has been independently verified is [Jeanne Calment](/wiki/Jeanne_Calment "Jeanne Calment") (1875–1997\) of France, who lived to the age of 122 years and 164 days.[\[b]](#cite_note-Calment-6) The oldest verified man ever is [Jiroemon Kimura](/wiki/Jiroemon_Kimura "Jiroemon Kimura") (1897–2013\) of Japan, who lived to the age of 116 years and 54 days.


The oldest known living person is [Tomiko Itooka](/wiki/Tomiko_Itooka "Tomiko Itooka") of Japan, aged 116 years, 160 days.[\[5]](#cite_note-WSRL-7) The oldest known living man is [John Tinniswood](/wiki/John_Tinniswood "John Tinniswood") of the United Kingdom, aged 112 years, 65 days.[\[6]](#cite_note-8) The 100 oldest women have, on average, lived several years longer than the 100 oldest men.
