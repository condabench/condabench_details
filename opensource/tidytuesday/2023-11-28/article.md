From Wikipedia, the free encyclopedia


TV series episode list
This article is about the episodes starting from 2005\. For the 1963–1989 episodes and 1996 film, see [List of *Doctor Who* episodes (1963–1989\)](/wiki/List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(1963%E2%80%931989) "List of Doctor Who episodes (1963–1989)").



*[Doctor Who](/wiki/Doctor_Who "Doctor Who")* is a British [science fiction television](/wiki/Science_fiction_on_television "Science fiction on television") programme produced by the [BBC](/wiki/BBC "BBC"). As of 22 June 2024,[\[update]](https://en.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(2005%E2%80%93present)&action=edit) 883 episodes of *Doctor Who* have aired. This includes one television movie and multiple specials, and encompasses 311 stories over 40 seasons, starting in 1963\. Additionally, four charity specials and two animated serials have also been aired. The programme's high episode count has resulted in *Doctor Who* holding the [world record](/wiki/World_record "World record") for the highest number of episodes of a science\-fiction programme.[\[1]](#cite_note-record-1) As of November 2023, up to a seventeenth series of the revived era has been planned.[\[2]](#cite_note-PlansSeason3&4-2)


*Doctor Who* ceased broadcasting in 1989, then [resumed in 2005](/wiki/History_of_Doctor_Who#Ninth_Doctor "History of Doctor Who"). The original series (1963–1989\), generally consists of multi\-episode serials. The 2005 revival trades the earlier serial format for a run of self\-contained episodes, interspersed with occasional multi\-part stories and structured into loose story arcs.



The story numbers below are meant as a guide to placement in the overall context of the programme.[\[3]](#cite_note-doctorwhotvstories-3) There is some dispute, for instance, about whether to count Season 23's *[The Trial of a Time Lord](/wiki/The_Trial_of_a_Time_Lord "The Trial of a Time Lord")* as one or as four serials,[\[4]](#cite_note-4) and whether the unfinished serial *[Shada](/wiki/Shada_(Doctor_Who) "Shada (Doctor Who)")* should be included.[\[5]](#cite_note-5) The numbering scheme in this list follows the official website's episode guide.[\[3]](#cite_note-doctorwhotvstories-3) Other sources, such as the [Region 1](/wiki/DVD_region_code#1 "DVD region code") classic *Doctor Who* [DVD releases](/wiki/List_of_Doctor_Who_DVD_releases "List of Doctor Who DVD releases"), use different numbering schemes, which diverge after the 108th story, *[The Horns of Nimon](/wiki/The_Horns_of_Nimon "The Horns of Nimon")* (1979–1980\).

### Ninth Doctor

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(2005%E2%80%93present)&action=edit&section=6 "Edit section: Ninth Doctor")]
In 2005, the BBC relaunched *[Doctor Who](/wiki/Doctor_Who "Doctor Who")* after a 15\-year absence from episodic television, with [Russell T Davies](/wiki/Russell_T_Davies "Russell T Davies"), [Julie Gardner](/wiki/Julie_Gardner "Julie Gardner"), and [Mal Young](/wiki/Mal_Young "Mal Young") as executive producers, [Phil Collinson](/wiki/Phil_Collinson "Phil Collinson") as producer, and [Christopher Eccleston](/wiki/Christopher_Eccleston "Christopher Eccleston") taking the lead role of the [Ninth Doctor](/wiki/Ninth_Doctor "Ninth Doctor").


For the first time since the 1965–1966 season, each episode has a title, and most were stand alone as complete stories. The show also returned to its traditional Saturday evening slot.


During Eccleston's tenure, the episodes were all set on either Earth, or in its orbit, in its past, present and future. In his last story, *[The Parting of the Ways](/wiki/The_Parting_of_the_Ways "The Parting of the Ways")*, the Doctor absorbed the Time Vortex to save his companion, leading to his [regeneration](/wiki/Regeneration_(Doctor_Who) "Regeneration (Doctor Who)").

### Tenth Doctor

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(2005%E2%80%93present)&action=edit&section=8 "Edit section: Tenth Doctor")]

### Eleventh Doctor

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(2005%E2%80%93present)&action=edit&section=13 "Edit section: Eleventh Doctor")]
The [Eleventh Doctor](/wiki/Eleventh_Doctor "Eleventh Doctor") was portrayed by [Matt Smith](/wiki/Matt_Smith "Matt Smith"). [Steven Moffat](/wiki/Steven_Moffat "Steven Moffat") took over as showrunner from the fifth series, swapping the melodramatic stories of his predecessor for ones involving convoluted time travel, which remained a staple for the entirety of Smith's tenure as the Doctor.[\[16]](#cite_note-28)[\[17]](#cite_note-29) Smith retained the role until the Christmas special *[The Time of the Doctor](/wiki/The_Time_of_the_Doctor "The Time of the Doctor")*, with the Doctor dying due to old age and fatigue from a centuries\-long war.[\[18]](#cite_note-30)

### Twelfth Doctor

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(2005%E2%80%93present)&action=edit&section=18 "Edit section: Twelfth Doctor")]
The [Twelfth Doctor](/wiki/Twelfth_Doctor "Twelfth Doctor") was portrayed by [Peter Capaldi](/wiki/Peter_Capaldi "Peter Capaldi"). His tenure saw a reduction of total episodes per series from thirteen to twelve. His Doctor was characterised by his grappling with the question of the meaning of goodness and whether he is himself a good man. Capaldi played the role until the Christmas special [Twice Upon a Time](/wiki/Twice_Upon_a_Time_(Doctor_Who) "Twice Upon a Time (Doctor Who)"), trying to save a ship of strangers from Cybermen, "without hope, without witness, without reward".

### Thirteenth Doctor

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(2005%E2%80%93present)&action=edit&section=22 "Edit section: Thirteenth Doctor")]
The [Thirteenth Doctor](/wiki/Thirteenth_Doctor "Thirteenth Doctor") was portrayed by [Jodie Whittaker](/wiki/Jodie_Whittaker "Jodie Whittaker"),[\[20]](#cite_note-ThirteenthDoctorCasting-32) becoming the first woman to play the role, with [Chris Chibnall](/wiki/Chris_Chibnall "Chris Chibnall") taking over as showrunner from the eleventh series onwards, and the reduction of episodes from twelve to ten. They both departed in the special [The Power of the Doctor](/wiki/The_Power_of_the_Doctor "The Power of the Doctor"), with the Doctor dying at the hands of the Master.

Main article: [*Doctor Who* specials (2023\)](/wiki/Doctor_Who_specials_(2023) "Doctor Who specials (2023)")
These specials celebrated Doctor Who's 60th anniversary. They reunited the Doctor with Donna Noble; introduced [The Meep](/wiki/Beep_the_meep "Beep the meep") on\-screen; involved thwarting a threat at the universe's edge; re\-introduced The Toymaker; and concluded with the Fourteenth Doctor's unique bi\-generation.

### Fifteenth Doctor

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(2005%E2%80%93present)&action=edit&section=29 "Edit section: Fifteenth Doctor")]
The [Fifteenth Doctor](/wiki/Fifteenth_Doctor "Fifteenth Doctor") is portrayed by [Ncuti Gatwa](/wiki/Ncuti_Gatwa "Ncuti Gatwa") and is the current Doctor.[\[27]](#cite_note-40)[\[28]](#cite_note-41)[\[25]](#cite_note-Tennant14Gatwa15b-38)
His tenure saw the reduction of episodes from ten to eight and is the first series to be distributed worldwide outside the UK by [Disney\+](/wiki/Disney%2B "Disney+").



#### Series 14 (2024\)

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(2005%E2%80%93present)&action=edit&section=30 "Edit section: Series 14 (2024)")]
Main article: [*Doctor Who* series 14](/wiki/Doctor_Who_series_14 "Doctor Who series 14")
The Christmas special introduced [Millie Gibson](/wiki/Millie_Gibson "Millie Gibson") as companion [Ruby Sunday](/wiki/Ruby_Sunday "Ruby Sunday"), a foundling experiencing luck caused by the antics of goblins, while the following season centred around the mystery of Ruby's birth, as well as a mysterious woman who appeared in different forms within each of the Doctor and Ruby's adventures. The series released at midnight on [BBC iPlayer](/wiki/BBC_iPlayer "BBC iPlayer") in the UK, with the BBC broadcast happening the following Saturday evening.[\[29]](#cite_note-42)

#### Series 15

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(2005%E2%80%93present)&action=edit&section=31 "Edit section: Series 15")]

A Christmas special will air on 25 December 2024, followed by the eight\-episode fifteenth series in 2025\.[\[6]](#cite_note-SeriesPlans-13) [Millie Gibson](/wiki/Millie_Gibson "Millie Gibson") is expected to return as [Ruby Sunday](/wiki/Ruby_Sunday "Ruby Sunday"), alongside new companion, Belinda Chandra, played by [Varada Sethu](/wiki/Varada_Sethu "Varada Sethu").[\[31]](#cite_note-45) Filming occurred between 23 October 2023 and 25 May 2024\.[\[32]](#cite_note-46)

#### Series 16

\[[edit](/w/index.php?title=List_of_Doctor_Who_episodes_(2005%E2%80%93present)&action=edit&section=32 "Edit section: Series 16")]
A third series with Davies as showrunner was being planned by November 2023\.[\[2]](#cite_note-PlansSeason3&4-2) Gatwa commented on returning in April 2024\.[\[35]](#cite_note-49) By that June, Davies was working on the fourth script for the series, despite it not yet being commissioned, with recording expected to begin in early 2025, and an expected release within 2026\.[\[36]](#cite_note-50)
