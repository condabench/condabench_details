GB warm up for World Series with Elche win
------------------------------------------



 Great Britain were faultless winners of the traditional European warm\-up tournament before the World Series last weekend. Spain \- the other European world series team in the event \- were runners up after an early stumble.

Argentina defend South American 7s title
----------------------------------------



 After winning their first title last year against a less experienced Brazilian team, this year Argentina's Yaguaretés showed that was no fluke by defeating a more experienced team from Brazil, a regular HSBC SVNS team, in the final.

Germany open Trophy campaign with win
-------------------------------------



 A big win for Germany against Finland opened the 2024/5 European Trophy. In the first half Germany tried to compete with the Finnish forwards allowing them to control too much of the game in the first half, but in the second half they gave their backs free rein and ran in six tries against a tiring Finnish defence. 




 See more](https://www.scrumqueens.com/news/germany-open-trophy-campaign-with-win)

England retain WXV1 title
-------------------------



 England were forced to dig deep to retain their WXV 1 title with a 21\-12 victory against hosts Canada at BC Place. Earlier, New Zealand winger Katelyn Vahaakolo scored a hat\-trick to help New Zealand to their first win of the tournament, beating France 39\-14\. 




 See more](https://www.scrumqueens.com/news/england-retain-wxv1-title)

Junior ‘Boks win African U20 series
-----------------------------------



 The Junior Springbok Women finished the R10 African U20 Women’s Series on a high by beating Uganda 35\-3 in their third and final fixture Tuesday afternoon at the Markötter Stadium in Stellenbosch. 




 See more](https://www.scrumqueens.com/news/junior-boks-win-african-u20-series)


[News \| October 6, 2024 \| 
 news



England v Canada for WXV1 title
-------------------------------



 Defending champions England set up a WXV 1 title decider against hosts Canada at the end of a thrilling second round at Langley Event Centre. The Red Roses ran in nine tries to beat Women’s Rugby World Cup 2021 winners New Zealand 49\-31 and maintain their 100 per cent record in the top level. 




 See more](https://www.scrumqueens.com/news/england-v-canada-for-wxv1-title)
