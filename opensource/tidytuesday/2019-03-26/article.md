Seattle’s most popular pet names of 2018
========================================



Hello (again), Lucy!




 
 By
 
[Sarah Anne Lloyd](https://seattle.curbed.com/authors/sarah-anne-lloyd)[@sarahannelloyd](https://www.twitter.com/sarahannelloyd)



 Jan 2, 2019, 1:14pm PST





















SeaRick1/Shutterstock



As we ring in 2019, Seattle has a lot to look back on from the last year. We [had that whole thing with the “head tax.”](https://seattle.curbed.com/2018/6/11/17451276/seattle-head-tax-employee-hours) We saw our [bike\-share landscape](https://seattle.curbed.com/2017/6/27/15856446/seattle-bikeshare-companies-bikes-specs-launch) get turned inside out. We [airlifted some goats](https://seattle.curbed.com/2018/10/1/17924992/goats-olympics-cascades-park-removal) out of the Olympics. We [got a hockey team](https://seattle.curbed.com/2018/12/4/18125878/seattle-nhl-key-arena-remodel), and our baseball stadium [got a new name](https://seattle.curbed.com/2018/12/19/18148770/mariners-field-new-name-t-mobile-park). An [addition to the Convention Center](https://seattle.curbed.com/2018/5/8/17331934/convention-center-city-council-vote) broke ground, and we [braced for the demise](https://seattle.curbed.com/2018/9/17/17871686/alaskan-way-viaduct-closure-date) of the Alaskan Way Viaduct.


And, [just like last year](https://seattle.curbed.com/2017/12/15/16783234/seattle-popular-pet-names-2017), we adopted pets, and we named those pets. And out of those adopted pets, more than 33,000 dogs, cats, goats, and potbellied pigs were licensed with the city in 2018\. (In addition to introducing your pet to the city, [this is technically the law](https://www.seattle.gov/animal-shelter/license).)


Among these (usually) furry friends, some names popped up more than others. Lucy was the most popular pet name overall and No. 1 for dogs. Next come Charlie and Luna—the latter being the No. 1 for cats.


For the most part, the top names were similar in 2018 to those in 2017, but there were a few shakeups. Lucy remains the reigning champ for most popular dog name, but Luna rose to the top for cats. Some names that were relatively popular before shot up in popularity this year: Milo, for example, rose up 13 places to break the top 20\. Penny, Cooper, Stella, Daisy, and Lola were also more popular than they were last year. Some were a little less popular: Chloe and Zoe both descended the ranks, along with Maggie and Molly.

While many popular names for dogs are popular names for cats too, there’s a little less similarity compared to last year, which saw Lucy as No. 1 for both. Lucy has fallen to third for cats for 2018, with Luna and Max each rising a place to No. 1 and No. 2, respectively.

In case you were wondering: Three dogs were, in fact, named Kitty, compared to 26 actual kitties. Two dogs and one cat were named Puppy. One dog was named Doggy. One dog was named Horse, and one cat was named Seahorse.


Out of the 31 livestock that were registered with the city—helpfully separated into goats and pigs this year—[each had a unique name](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1fVQRs_OnqTKB6Ounva7bp3_0IDl7U-Eomw33wOZEOko/edit?usp=sharing); Professor Nibblesworth and Brussels Sprout had their licenses renewed this year. One pig was named Coconut. 


The biggest name that transcends species: Max, with 124 dogs, 54 cats, and one goat.
